Research readiness assessment — 2026-10-29

The updated study is concrete enough to support an inspectable working paper.
It has native public-implementation evidence, actual client-process cuts, raw
model outputs, conditional analysis, targeted controls, separate evidence
checkers, negative fixtures, pinned public source and computational reproduction.
The original primary source/cases remain immutable. No evidence is fabricated.

The strongest candidate contribution is narrower than a new memory system:
a candidate-derived exact-hash admission boundary, live replay witnesses, and
measured literal-context redundancy while all exact query targets remain present.
At N40/K10 the native retry state supplies 247 summed per-query distinct strings
in 400 returned slots, versus 400 in baseline/controls. It is not a QA accuracy or
semantic-harm result. All four sizes and all three tested budgets are retained.

A top-journal contribution is not established. The N−k bound is elementary;
public Mem0 discussion already covers bounded-candidate semantic duplicates;
durable replay, receipts, epochs, snapshot anomalies and lifecycle evaluation
have close prior art. The two MemTX paper/code boundary observations may be
implementation or documentation defects pending original-author clarification,
and do not refute the separate action/repair invariants. Redis runtime-error
behavior is known provider semantics. More cells cannot convert these facts
into algorithmic novelty or prove priority.

The live evidence uses one requested model/harness, four authored source batches,
bounded/truncated history and no independent semantic adjudication. Retrieval is
a post hoc exact-self-query mechanism probe on selected fresh-history stores.
There is no held-out natural-workload incidence, answer-generation task, latency,
cost, downstream harm, remote cluster or power-loss evaluation. Same-author
checking and hosted computational reproduction do not replace outside replication.

The next study capable of materially improving the journal case should freeze
held-out workloads and independent semantic labels, test additional independent
memory systems, and evaluate a scalable concurrent uniqueness/admission contract
against strong existing implementations and useful downstream outcomes. It
should preserve negative results and compute uncertainty only for an appropriate
sampling design. These are research requirements, not evidence already collected.

The current manuscript is 19 pages with 54 substantively selected references. This
assessment is an AI-assisted critical review of supplied evidence, not external
peer review, an acceptance score or a publication forecast. No journal submission,
DOI, university research affiliation or citation/h-index outcome is asserted.
